About Me

I'm a CNC programmer and machinist who enjoys figuring out better ways to make parts. Whether it's improving a CNC program, building a fixture, or automating a repetitive task, I like finding solutions that make the job easier and more consistent.

Over the years I've developed a strong focus on process improvement, repeatability, and efficiency. My goal is to build reliable processes that produce quality parts, reduce setup time, and keep production running smoothly.

Outside of machining, I enjoy software development and automation. I primarily work with Python and build tools that solve real problems on the shop floor, from production dashboards and scheduling systems to utilities that eliminate manual work. I enjoy learning new technologies and applying them where they provide practical value.
