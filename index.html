About Me

I’m a CNC Programmer with hands-on machining experience and a strong focus on process control. My goal is to build programs that are repeatable, efficient, and stable in production environments.

I continuously refine processes to reduce cycle time, control variation, and eliminate scrap. I also leverage automation and software where it adds value, bringing engineering-level thinking to the shop floor.
